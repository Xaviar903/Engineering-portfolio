#### Finding 3: Different plastic types

**What I found:**  
I researched what plastics we need to worry about and how they all work.
1- PET / PETE (Polyethylene Terephthalate): is strong lightweight and moisture resistant.
2- HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene): is Chemical and moisture resistant as well as durable and rigid.
3- PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride): is Highly biological and chemical resistant, and rigid or flexible.
4- LDPE (Low-Density Polyethylene): is Highly flexible tough translucent and can be easy to seal.
5- PP (Polypropylene): has a high melting point and highly chemical and heat resistant.
6- PS (Polystyrene): Has a low melting point and can be expanded into foam.
7- Number seven is just mixtures between the above types.
**Why it matters to our project:**  
This can help us find ways for each one of them to be recycled and can tell us what they are resistant to.
